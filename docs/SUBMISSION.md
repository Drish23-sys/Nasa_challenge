## Project name
Earth Analog Finder

## One-line summary
An interactive, explainable tool that ranks places on Earth by how well they rehearse a chosen Moon or Mars site, built from open NASA data.

## The problem
Mission teams pick analog sites by reputation, and many analog sites are not well characterized. There is no simple, open way to compare a
target site against candidate Earth sites using measurable criteria, or to discover lesser-known sites.

## What we built
- Target selector: Shackleton rim and Malapert Massif (candidate lunar base areas), Jezero and Gale (Mars).
- 22 Earth candidate sites, including 12+ lesser-known ones (e.g. Danakil, Salar de Uyuni, Dasht-e Lut, Tibesti, Pinacate, Svalbard, Lava Beds).
- Five-factor explainable similarity score with adjustable weights, environment filters and side-by-side comparison.
- For every site: why it is an analog **and where the analogy breaks down**.
- AI explanations of each match, grounded only in the data shown.

## How the score works
Normalized factors (slope, roughness, temperature extremes, aridity, basaltic geology). Similarity per factor = 1 - |target - site|; overall = weighted
average. Weights are user-adjustable. All normalization constants are in `backend/pipeline/build_sites.py` and published in `meta.normalization`.

## Limitations (we state these in the app)
Earth cannot reproduce reduced gravity, vacuum, thin atmosphere, radiation. Temperature and aridity are proxies. Geology is hand-tagged. Curated set, not a global search.
